The Fool's Court

The printed edition — everything you need on paper

  • Rules sheet The complete rules in ten numbered sections, set in two columns with a masthead, worked scoring examples and a summary of play.
  • Whisper cards All fifteen Whispers as poker-size cards, nine to a sheet, with a matching back.
  • Quick reference card Two-sided at A6. Making a pledge on one side; who holds sway and winning favour on the other.

Print everything at 100% scale with margins set to none or minimum — the sheets carry their own margins. Every page is generated from the game's own rules files, so the printed edition and the app can never disagree.
