Whisper cards

Twenty-two Whisper cards, nine to a sheet at poker size (63.5 × 88.9 mm), followed by a sheet of matching backs. Print at 100% scale on card stock, then cut along the hairlines.

To make them double-sided, print the three front sheets, then the back sheet onto the reverse of each. Five of the nine backs on the last sheet will be spare.
