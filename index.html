How bidding works

Choose three cards and lay them face down. Their suits add up to the number of tricks you are bidding — the ranks are ignored.

  • ♠ Spades 3
  • ♥ Hearts 2
  • ♦ Diamonds 1
  • ♣ Clubs 0

Your bid stays secret until the hand ends, and those three cards are out of play — so ten tricks are up for grabs and the highest possible bid is nine.

How trump is chosen

Trump for each hand depends on how many of the four players made their bid exactly in the hand before.

  • 0 players → ♣ Clubs
  • 1 player → ♦ Diamonds
  • 2 players → ♥ Hearts
  • 3 players → ♠ Spades
  • 4 players → No Trump

The first hand of every game is played at No Trump.

How scoring works
  • Bid exactly right — 2 points for every trick you won.
  • Bid wrong — 1 point per trick won, minus 2 for every trick you were off.
  • Bid zero — +5 if you take no tricks at all, −5 if you take any.

Bid 5 and win 4 → 4 − 2 = 2. Bid 3 and win 6 → 6 − 6 = 0.
