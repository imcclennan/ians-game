The Fool's Court

Favour

Making a pledge

Send four agents out of the room on secret errands. Which kind you send is your pledge — how many audiences you promise to win. Their standing at court counts for nothing here.

  • ♠ Assassin 3
  • ♥ Lover 2
  • ♦ Merchant 1
  • ♣ Fool 0

Nobody sees your pledge until the session ends, and the agents you sent are gone for it — so eleven audiences remain to be won. Four Assassins come to twelve, which is more than the court has to give: promise that and your word is already broken.

Who holds sway

Sway passes according to how many of the four nobles kept their pledge exactly in the session before.

  • 0 nobles → ♣ Fools
  • 1 noble → ♦ Merchants
  • 2 nobles → ♥ Lovers
  • 3 nobles → ♠ Assassins
  • 4 nobles → No Sway

When every noble breaks their word the Fool rules the court; when all four keep it, no one holds sway at all. The opening session of a season is always No Sway.

Winning favour
  • Pledge kept exactly — 2 favour for every audience you won.
  • Pledge broken — 1 favour per audience won, less 2 for every audience you were off.
  • Pledged nothing — +5 for taking no audience at all. Take one and it is −5, then −2 more for every audience after.

Pledge 5, win 4 → 4 − 2 = 2. Pledge 3, win 6 → 6 − 6 = 0.
