Three separate orders: PrinterStudio prints one back per deck.

**Every file is 900 × 1500 px JPEG, quality 96, 4:4:4, sRGB** — 3.00″ × 5.00″ at
300 DPI, which is the 2.75″ × 4.75″ tarot trim plus 1/8″ bleed on every edge.
Place each at 100% and leave it; the bleed is already in the file.

## The deck

Enumerated from the game's own `COPIES` table, not from a loop over 1–15. The
four kinds do not share a ladder:

| Agent | How the kind is arranged | Cards |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Assassins | 11, 2×12, 3×13, 4×14, 5×15 | 15 |
| Lovers | 1, then 2× every even rank to 14 | 15 |
| Merchants | 1–15, one of each | 15 |
| Fools | 5×1, 4×2, 3×3, 2×4, 5 | 15 |

33 distinct agents, 60 physical cards. Copies of a rank share a face and are
told apart only by the filename — `deck-56_Assassin-15_1of5` — so the printer
gets sixty files and the table gets sixty agents identical in play. Upload the
whole folder; the numeric prefix keeps them in deal order.

Each face carries pips beside the rank saying how many of that agent the deck
holds, two to a row, up to five. Merchants show their single pip.
